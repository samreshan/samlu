    Permission 1 of 2

    Let Samlu hear you.

    Recording only happens while you hold the shortcut. Audio goes to the provider you configure — nowhere else.

    Microphone Records globally, even while another app is focused. Allowed

    macOS will show its own dialog. Samlu never records outside the shortcut.

    Permission 2 of 2

    Let Samlu put text back.

    Accessibility lets Samlu return text to the exact field where recording began. Without it, results are copied instead.

    Accessibility Automatic insertion into the app you were already using. Allowed

    You can skip this. Samlu will copy results to the clipboard until it's granted.
